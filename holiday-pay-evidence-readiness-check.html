Holiday Pay Evidence Readiness Check

A 2-minute check for payroll, HR and advisers preparing for Fair Work Agency holiday pay enforcement.

Why this matters: DBT is consulting on Fair Work Agency holiday pay compliance and enforcement from 2027. This check is not legal advice. It helps employers and advisers think about whether holiday pay records could evidence compliance.
1.Can the employer identify who was entitled to holiday across the relevant period?
2.Can the employer show how holiday entitlement was calculated?
3.Can the employer evidence holiday taken, paid, carried over or corrected?
4.Can the employer show how holiday pay was calculated and paid?
5.Can the employer evidence or reconstruct the records across six years?
